Pantanal seasonal trajectory selection

Every qualifying local burst, pooled across years within one season. Start with the seasonal totals below; open a collection for its animals, maps and temporal coverage. A collection contains computational map groups, each retaining every qualifying burst for its animals.

Main policy: observed fixes selected near a two-hour target (regular band 1.5–2.5 h), ≥3 selected fixes per burst, native and selected gaps ≤4.25 h (four hours plus 15 minutes jitter). Tag changes and season boundaries are cuts. Actual durations and all native observations within admitted bursts are preserved. There is no weekly density, weekly span, or affected-point percentage gate.

Interpretation: these are temporally eligible observations. Residence, habitat fidelity, random missingness and reward-model validity have not been established. A defined turn requires two nonzero observed displacements; it is not an effective independent sample count. Grid collapse and optimizer suitability are evaluated separately at export.

Season dates: Winter: May 15–August 14 — dry with rare showers. Spring: August 15–November 14 — hottest season of the year. Summer: November 15–March 21 — hot, humid, and rainy, with intense downpours or thunderstorms. Autumn: March 22–May 14 — gradual decrease in temperature and rain; rivers return to their banks. Summer’s season-year is its November start year. No season buffer. Source and user-approved operational dates: Climates to Travel.
