# Hofmann-style iSSA and options for jaguar route learning

Discussion dated 2026-11-02. **Research proposals, not implemented or approved changes.** This note changes no processor, selection thresholds, reports, export contracts, or model splits. The accepted route-learning specification remains in [pantanal_sampling_time_and_grid.md](pantanal_sampling_time_and_grid.md), with shared-reward training described in [biome_shared_reward_training.md](biome_shared_reward_training.md).

## What the paper establishes

[Hofmann, Cozzi and Fieberg (2024)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11081933/) compares methods for incomplete iSSA records. Dynamic+Model samples duration-specific step lengths and turning angles, then includes duration interactions with movement descriptors. Multistep concatenates nominal-duration random moves and uses their net endpoints; it requires integer duration multiples. Both improved movement estimation in the simulations; Dynamic+Model was recommended when movement parameters matter. Remaining turn-angle bias may reflect omitted previous-step duration. The hyena example reports improved precision and two-hour movement estimates despite using longer steps. The simulations assumed random missingness and an unchanging movement kernel; they do not establish robustness to habitat-dependent fix failure. The authors discuss duration-dependent habitat selection and diminishing gains beyond forgiveness two. At a two-hour nominal interval, a four-hour maximum is forgiveness two; this does not specify our endpoint-percentage, fragment-loss, or window-density rules.

Paper locations: “Fitting distributions,” “Generating random steps,” “Case study,” and “Discussion.” Author code is cited at [DRUM, DOI 10.13020/6wcd-6s43](https://doi.org/10.13020/6wcd-6s43). The article was inspected; the repository did not resolve through the available access tools, so no claim here is based on inspecting its scripts.

## Proposed selection and preprocessing experiment

The user is already planning an iSSA comparison, including novel-environment and interpolation evaluation. The question here is how to select usable jaguar observations. These are project proposals for that experiment, not instructions to modify the frozen processor.

1. **Audit valid steps and bouts throughout the buffered season.** Start from the raw movement CSV/full cleaned records: existing source-preserving checkpoints contain selected windows and cannot recover excluded records. For every one of the 56 animals, count timestamped candidate steps and valid consecutive-step pairs at the target scale before applying the current week-level admission rules. Preserve native fixes for validation and residence diagnostics, plus animal, tag, source-fix identity, season/year and provenance. The fine export preserves native sampling within selected windows; it does not reverse selection losses. Report how many animals have useful evidence even though no accepted seven-day window exists. This isolates “no usable movement observations” from “no week satisfying every operational rule.”
2. **Keep a two-hour reference and explicit gap classes.** A strict approximately two-hour analysis and a duration-aware analysis admitting steps through four hours would give a controlled comparison. Gaps above four hours and tag changes can still delineate bouts; retain eligible evidence on both sides. Longer gaps need not invalidate an entire week or animal. A 3.0-hour interval is not an exact four-hour step: inspect actual durations and justify a nominal-class tolerance or fit a parsimonious continuous-duration formulation. Preserve actual durations in both cases. Duration-aware estimates can be evaluated at two hours for a common reference.
3. **Count model-valid observations rather than equating them with fragment size.** Compute lengths and turning angles from projected observed coordinates within a bout. A directional step generally needs its predecessor, hence at least three sequential fixes for one defined turn; the first displacement in a burst lacks that information. This is a geometry requirement, not a claim that three fixes adequately characterize an animal. Five-observation fragments are an operational filter we could test independently. Genuine zero displacement also requires explicit treatment because heading and log length can be undefined. Preserve incoming and outgoing elapsed times for each angle; assess their influence before interpreting turns from mixed intervals.
4. **Test whole-window percentage gates independently.** The 20% affected-endpoint budget, 5% short-fragment-loss cap, minimum five observations, weekly schedule-density screen, and observed-span minimum answer different questions. For a duration-aware iSSA, a valid longer step can contribute even when many other steps in the same week are also long. Rejecting the whole week may therefore discard usable evidence. Test accepting all locally valid steps/bouts, with their duration information, against each existing gate. Do not replace these gates with another unexplained universal percentage. Require enough observed evidence for the intended parameterization and report uncertainty; random alternatives cannot create that evidence.
5. **Maintain two distinct representation contracts.** For iSSA preprocessing, use timestamped coordinates before same-cell compression or rasterized connecting cells. For plain MaxEnt, retain source-to-grid provenance and diagnose path ambiguity, inferred-edge burden and gap lengths. An observational displacement can inform endpoint selection without identifying the route traveled between endpoints. Successful endpoint inference is insufficient evidence that a linearly reconstructed route is a reliable positive route demonstration.
6. **Audit observation bias and sample-size gains separately.** Tabulate valid steps/bouts, animals, sites, sex, seasonal phase and years under each screen; examine timing and habitat associations of missing fixes where data permit. Compare identical held-out animals/cases first, then the enlarged cohort. Keep all evidence from one animal in its assigned fold and maintain the geographic-transfer experiment. Additional records increase evidence, not independent animals. Weighting or partial pooling needs an explicit population target.

The recommended first diagnostic is consequently a per-animal, per-season retention table: all source observations → two-hour-target observations → eligible intervals through four hours → defined-turn steps → currently admitted weeks. Its purpose is to identify which operational rule, if any, suppresses otherwise useful movement information. This proposal does not guarantee recovery of all 56 animals.

## How relevant is the hyena example?

The article's terrestrial-carnivore example used nominal two-hour nighttime fixes, artificially removed 25% of observations, allowed two-, four- and six-hour steps, and used 250-m endpoint covariates. These design details are reported in its “Case study” section; they do not establish a jaguar-specific threshold.

The useful connection is the observational problem: an animal moves between sparsely observed locations, and missing fixes change the duration represented by one displacement. Ecological similarity alone cannot establish a shared movement kernel, activity schedule, gap tolerance, habitat response, or spatial resolution. For these jaguars, examine duration-dependent lengths, turns, repeated locations, grid-crossing distances and habitat heterogeneity. The existing 500-m/two-hour sensitivity analysis is relevant empirical evidence for our representation choice; it does not demonstrate that a four-hour gap contains a known sequence of 500-m moves. Avoid importing the hyena's nighttime restriction or six-hour maximum merely because both species are carnivores.

## Highest-value experiment: realistic artificial missingness

Take the densest usable jaguar records as a finer observational reference. Specify animal/geographic splits first, then mask fixes with reproducible random deletion and clustered/block dropout across a prespecified severity range. Apply unchanged versus proposed preprocessing using only the remaining observations. Withheld coordinates must not determine which candidates pass, select a best window, tune gap cutoffs, or fit proposal distributions. Tune design choices within the development folds and reserve a locked evaluation set.

Evaluate recovery of the actual withheld fixes, spatial route deviations, observed-versus-inserted cell proportions, gap length, and retained animal/bout coverage. Compare errors at common held-out cases; separately report cases available only under the expanded policy. The dense record is still an incomplete GPS reference, not continuous-path truth. Artificial deletion also cannot establish that real missingness is independent of habitat; the block scenarios are stress tests, not a complete collar-failure model.

This tests our specific linear/raster bridging assumptions against jaguar evidence. The paper's continuous-time imputation experiment is a different procedure and does not directly prove that this project's raster interpolation is biased. Conversely, success at retaining duration-labelled endpoint observations does not validate a particular reconstructed path. Four hours corresponds to two nominal two-hour observation intervals; it never implies two spatial grid moves. A jaguar may remain within one 500-m cell or cross many cells during either interval.

For plain MaxEnt, increasing irregularity while leaving the likelihood and connecting-path assumptions unchanged requires this empirical check. A future timed movement kernel or marginalization over possible paths would be a separate model development, beyond a preprocessing relaxation.

## Choices worth resolving before implementation

- Should the first retention audit cover all buffered seasonal records, with the existing selected weeks retained as a reproducible reference cohort?
- Does the observed interval distribution justify nominal two-/four-hour classes, or require continuous-duration modeling? Answer this with timestamps before assigning classes.
- Which quality screens are necessary for each model's likelihood, and which only enforce convenient uniform record lengths? Test changes individually so added animals and changed evidence are attributable.
- Which common held-out observations and conditioning evidence will support the already-planned interpolation/novel-environment comparison? Raw next-step and destination-conditioned route likelihoods are different outcomes and should not be numerically compared as equivalent.

None of these sources provides a biological justification for this project's five-observation minimum, 20% affected-endpoint cap, 5% fragment-loss cap, or seven-day envelope. Those remain operational assumptions to test against retention and out-of-sample performance.

## Appendix: what random alternatives mean

[Avgar et al. (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.12528) jointly estimates movement and habitat selection using conditional logistic regression. This addresses the dependence of observed movement distributions on habitat selection: a distribution fitted directly to observed displacements is a starting proposal, not automatically the fitted habitat-independent movement kernel.

[Michelot et al. (2024), §2.2.3](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.14248) clarifies the numerical-integration interpretation. Random endpoints approximate the normalizing integral of the step-selection density. A proposal distribution that oversamples some endpoints requires the corresponding importance-sampling correction; its support must cover the modeled domain. The proposal and fitted movement kernel are distinct objects. Their paper also discusses deterministic quadrature as an alternative when the spatial integral is tractable.

For our purposes, an observed displacement and its alternatives would form one matched choice set. They share the observed start and observation interval. Only the observed displacement is the realized choice; alternatives are integration points. They are not additional jaguar observations, independent animals, or positive expert demonstrations for MaxEnt IRL. Calling all alternatives ecological “absences” would also overstate what they establish.

The official [`amt` SSF vignette](https://jmsigner.r-universe.dev/amt/doc/p4_SSF.html) demonstrates `steps_by_burst`, `random_steps`, covariate extraction, and stratified model fitting. It supplies gamma and von Mises proposal defaults and allows alternatives. Those defaults need distributional checks for these jaguars; they are not a universal biological law.
