# Literature basis for seasonal whole-animal holdout

Updated 2026-11-04. The current [reusable methodology](seasonal_holdout_methodology_20261104.md)
is authoritative. Original animals are the sole split unit; map groups are
computational tags and impose no quotas or local-support constraints. The earlier
group-stratified proposal is withdrawn. Its numerical audit is historical only.

Repeated observations within an animal do not constitute independent animals.
[Fieberg et al.2010](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2894958/) discusses
correlation and individual heterogeneity in habitat selection.
[Roberts et al.2017](https://doi.org/10.1111/ecog.02881) explains matching structured
validation to prediction targets.
[Northrup et al.2022](https://doi.org/10.1002/eap.2470) supports whole-individual
withholding when evaluating transfer to unsampled individuals.
[Cawley and Talbot2010](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v11/cawley10a.html) explains
model-selection bias and why final test outcomes must not select the pipeline.

These principles support animal blocking, not a theoretically optimal80/20 ratio.
Spring34/9 is a pragmatic training/assessment compromise. Sample9 from all43
uniformly without replacement; singleton-map animals are equally eligible. Report
animal-level scores and their mean; do not apply the withdrawn group-quota weights.
Geographic support remains a diagnostic, and boundary/domain conditioning must
be disclosed. Separate isolated seasonal fits need no temporal-transfer experiment
to justify their allocations. Global allocation infeasibility has not been proved.
No ecological validation or animal draw is claimed by this literature note.
